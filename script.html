Rumah Sayang Voice Script

Skrip Rakaman Suara

90 short Bahasa Melayu lines for Rumah Sayang, in 8 sections. Each section is one recording, about 1–2 minutes long. When a line has been recorded, the game plays your voice instead of the tablet's voice.

0 of 8 sections recorded

How to record

  1. Find a quiet, soft room. A bedroom with curtains and a bed works well. Turn off the fan and air-conditioner and close the windows.
  2. Use your phone's voice recorder. Hold it about a hand's length (20–30 cm) from your mouth, slightly to the side.
  3. Speak warmly and a little slower than normal, as if talking to a 4-year-old. Smile while you speak; children can hear it.
  4. One recording per section. Read the lines in order. Pause for about 2 seconds between lines (count "satu, dua" silently). Don't read out the line numbers.
  5. If you make a mistake, stop and record that section again from the start. Each section is short.
  6. Name each file by its section letter: A, B … H. Any format is fine (m4a, mp3, wav, ogg).
  7. Attach the files in our chat. I'll split them into single lines, clean up background noise, even out the volume and put them into the game.

You can send sections as you finish them. Lines that aren't recorded yet keep using the tablet's voice, so the game always works.

    You are getting so good at this! Let's try something a little harder.

    They like your gentle touch. Which choice shows love?

    This friend is happy right now. Look for the friend with a bubble.
